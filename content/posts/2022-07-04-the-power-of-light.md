You see the light interact as particles, and like when gazing at the stars and the universe — if you zoom out enough the light changes —, this means that as you back out further, the light you see plays with itself and changes according to its own nature. It becomes something else.

Light is something quite interesting: we have realized that it can behave like a particle, but it can also behave like a wave. This is so incredible, this means that light lives in some sort of duality, where sometimes it's fixated, and some other times it's moving.

### Definition of Light, Energy, Gravity 

According to Wikipedia, "light is a form of electromagnetic radiation with a wavelength which can be detected by the human eye". This means that light is basically energy that travels through space, in a specified frequency. The human eye can only perceive the visible spectrum, yet that only represents around 1% of all available wavelengths.     

It's like a TV signal, but we can only access 1% of the channels that are being broadcast.

Light is everything that we currently see and it's the fastest speed that we can get to. Something moving faster than light would mean that we would not see it, since it would be vibrating with higher wavelengths, thus, unable to be perceived by the human eye.

Light is energy, warmth... it's love, it's the energy that binds us together, literally. The sun emits this energy as it slowly dies, but that's the energy that is holding us alive in space, held in it's gravity.

I believe that it is amazing that we have found ourselves in the third planet of a small orange Dwarf. It's also quite interesting to know that the same star that gives us energy and life, would be able to cause total death and dispair. 

What does that even mean? It means that reality is more intricate than what we think. 

### How Light Works, How Light Creates the World 

I know that it might seem obvious, but we have to keep into accont that thanks to the light that we get from the sun we are able to develop our human life.

The light particles emanated from the sun take about 8 minutes to reach the earth. Once the light reaches our planet, it hits the atmosphere and travels down to the surface, where it bounces off and hits our eyes. 

Once light reaches our eyes, it is processed and absorbed by the iris, from each eye, and then this information travels from our eyes towards the brain, where all of this information gets processed, realy quickly, and our world is created. This whole process takes less than a second.

The world is created during every single second, over and over again. 

Following our previous anecdote, since the light that has bounced off from the ground into our iris takes some time to be processed by our brains, we're essentially seeing the past, at least a few miliseconds in the past.

That _light_ is what keeps us all warm, it's what allow us to see, to diferentiate objects one from another. That's the power of light.

Being warmth is just the movement of particles going faster, there is more excitation due to the temperature and things are moving fast and quick.

This is what being warm is and it is what the sun is meant to do: excite, illuminate, keep in the light, warm. Plants do photosynthesis to get their nutrients. They absord them from light itself.

We are all bound by light, we all need it. 

Light is that warmth that keeps us alive. It's what allows plants to breath and carry on, generating oxigen for us to live at the same time. 

It's the warmth that keeps us alive when coldness invades. 

It's what allows us to be here, since it made way for us to exist.

We emanated from light, since it's what essentially allowed us to exist on this planet. The power of light is being the creator of life as we know it.
