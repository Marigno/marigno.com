THAT’S THEIR LIFE. 

What is your life? Wake up. 

That’s what they’ll keep doing and being, that’s what it means to be alive for them. 

What’s your meaning?

Don’t waste time longing and waiting for things to happen. 

Make them happen! 

«Keep moving towards the light!»
